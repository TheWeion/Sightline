# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first release.

- View distances follow your surroundings: long in the open, shorter in cities, indoors, in
  instances and underwater. Terrain and horizon drop the most, ground clutter and
  characters the least.
- Every subzone is calibrated to hold at least 60 FPS, or a target FPS you set, and is
  recalibrated if the frame rate drops there later.
- A background FPS cap is not mistaken for a drop while the game is alt-tabbed.
- One button sets every view-distance setting to the engine maximum, and can be undone.
- A window (`/sight`) with live values, every setting and the list of calibrations.
